# 🚴‍♂️ Brevly Labs

Welcome to the **Brevly Labs** organization on GitHub. 

We build the [Brevly Ecosystem](https://brevly.app), a distributed event tracking and registration system designed specifically for cycling brevet events. It focuses on extreme simplicity for riders out on the road (via Telegram and low-bandwidth mobile apps) and high efficiency for organizers.

## 🌟 The Brevly Ecosystem

Our goal is to modernize long-distance cycling events by removing the friction from registration, tracking, and validation, allowing riders to focus entirely on the route. The ecosystem is composed of several specialized, seamlessly integrated applications.

### 📱 For Riders
- **Telegram Bot Integration:** The core interface for participants. Quick registration, instant event updates, and seamless check-ins directly via Telegram, minimizing the need for heavy native apps.
- **Rider Dashboard:** A comprehensive web portal where riders can view their history, manage upcoming brevets, and analyze their performance.
- **Mini App (On-the-road):** A highly optimized, low-bandwidth Telegram Mini App designed for critical interactions during the ride, ensuring reliability even in areas with poor cellular coverage.

### 🏢 For Organizers
- **Organizer Hub:** A powerful, centralized dashboard for managing events, validating rider checkpoints in real-time, and handling post-event results and homologations.
- **Automated Workflows:** Dramatically reduces the manual administrative burden of running a brevet series, from registration fee collection to final result submission.
